I'm Feisal Onyango, a frontend-focused web developer based in Nairobi, Kenya, with two years of experience building interfaces that feel fast and behave the way people expect. Most of my work lands somewhere between "brochure site" and "small business's whole front-end," and I like it there: real users, real deadlines, real feedback within a week of shipping.

I specialize in frontend development — turning designs into responsive, accessible interfaces with React, JavaScript, and clean CSS. Below: what I do, what I've built, what past clients have said, and how to get in touch.
