I design & code clean interfaces

I’m Feisal, a frontend-focused web developer based in Nairobi, Kenya, with two years of experience building interfaces that feel fast and behave the way people expect. Most of my work lands somewhere between a brochure site and a small business’s entire front end — real users, real deadlines, feedback within a week of shipping.

I specialize in turning designs into responsive, accessible interfaces with React, JavaScript, and clean CSS — the kind of front end that still looks and works right two years after launch, on whatever device someone happens to be holding.
